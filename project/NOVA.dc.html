How we work

Four movements, six to twelve weeks. No handover cliff — the people who design it are the people who build it.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Interviews, audits and a hard look at what the business actually needs.

  2. 02

    Define

    A sharp strategy, a scope we can defend, and the one idea worth building.

  3. 03

    Build

    Design and engineering in the same room, shipping in weekly increments.

  4. 04

    Refine

    Measure, tune, and hand over something your team is proud to own.

A small studio, on purpose

NOVA is nine designers and engineers working from Lisbon and wherever our clients are. We take on six or seven engagements a year — enough to stay curious, few enough that a founder is always in the work.

We're at our best with teams who care about craft: fintechs, cultural institutions, and product companies past their first act.
